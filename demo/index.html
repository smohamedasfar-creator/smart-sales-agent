Price list

The Price & Stock agent checks every item here: the name, selling price, cost and stock.

Columns are found by their names in the first row, as in the real app: Item Code, Item Name or Description, Category, Unit, Selling Price, Cost Price, Stock or Qty. Without a stock column every item counts as available. The file stays in your browser.

Zoho: live stock from Zoho Books or Zoho Inventory is checked by the real app on your server, with your Zoho login. This page can't sign in to Zoho, so to test with Zoho items, export them to Excel from Zoho (Items, then Export) and upload that file here.
